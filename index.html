Why LitteRabbit? 🐰

Long story short... when trying to engrave "LittleRabbit" on an Apple Pencil, someone (not naming names) forgot a crucial "L".

And thus, "LitteRabbit" was born — slightly lighter in spelling, but twice as mysterious.

As for the true origin of Little Rabbit? That's for the legends to debate. Some say it's a childhood nickname. Others whisper of a secret bunny society. Only a few know the real story.

And they're not talking. 🐰
